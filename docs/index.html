eBay Listing Assistant

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This private application helps its owner prepare and manage listings on their own eBay seller account.

Information and purpose

The application uses information supplied by the owner and information returned by eBay to prepare listings, manage inventory and offers, and review seller policies. Seller authorization is used only to provide these functions.

Retention and sharing

The application retains the connection information needed to maintain authorized access. It does not maintain its own archive of eBay API responses, inventory records, orders, or buyer information. Results returned to a ChatGPT conversation are subject to the account holder’s ChatGPT settings and applicable provider policies. eBay and hosting providers may process operational data under their respective policies.

Owner control

The owner can remove the saved connection using the application’s connection controls and revoke the application’s access through eBay account settings. Reconnecting requires authorization again.

Service providers

The application uses eBay APIs and ChatGPT. Their respective privacy policies also apply to their services. This application is independent and is not operated by eBay.

Questions

This application is for its owner’s personal use. Privacy questions and requests can be raised directly with the owner through the same channel used to access the assistant.
